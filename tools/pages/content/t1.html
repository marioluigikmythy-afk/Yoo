How we handle your data

About T1

Is T1 a person?

No. T1 is software our team built to follow up on cases. Our human agents work with T1 and step in whenever a case needs a person.

How often does T1 contact Roblox?

Once a day for each open case, with an everyday follow-up message, until the case is verified and complete.

Does T1 send special or automated tricks?

No. T1 doesn't use special messages, shortcuts or loopholes. It sends the same kind of everyday follow-up a careful person would.

Does T1 need my password?

Never. T1 doesn't sign in to your account and never asks for passwords, verification codes or browser cookies.

Who runs T1?

Tranom built T1. It runs on third-party processors that host it under contract and may only use your information to run T1. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

What happens if something is missing?

We email you what Roblox needs and how to get it. Once you send it, T1 includes it in the next daily follow-up.
