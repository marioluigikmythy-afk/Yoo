Pricing questions

Do I pay before you look at my case?

No. Sending the form is free. We review your case, tell you whether it's Standard ({{STANDARD}}) or Priority ({{PRIORITY}}), and you pay only after you agree.

Are there any other fees?

No. The plan price is the whole price for your case. There are no subscriptions, renewals or extra charges.

Is Roblox Support really free?

Yes. Anyone can contact Roblox Support at no cost. You only need Tranom if you want help with proof, writing and daily follow-ups.

What if my account isn't recovered?

Only Roblox can restore an account, so we can't guarantee it. If you cancel before we start work, you get a full refund. After that, the fee covers the work we've done. See the refund promise.
