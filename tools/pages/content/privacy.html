The short version

  • We collect what you put in our recovery form and what you send us while we work on your case.
  • We never ask for your Roblox password, 2-Step Verification codes or browser cookies.

Who we are

Tranom ("Tranom", "we", "us") is a paid service that helps people recover access to their Roblox accounts through Roblox's official recovery and support channels. Tranom is operated by [Legal business name], [Business address].

We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Tranom is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation, and Roblox handles the information you give Roblox under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Recovery form: your name, email address, Roblox username, the type of problem, and your description of what happened.
  • Case materials: anything you choose to send us while we work on your case, such as purchase receipts, gift card details, screenshots, past usernames or the approximate date you created the account.
  • Messages: emails and other messages you exchange with us.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as the amount and the last four digits of a card, but never your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting provider records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Browser storage and cookies: described in Cookies and similar technologies.

What we never ask for

We will never ask for your Roblox password, your 2-Step Verification or backup codes, your email password, or your browser cookies (including any text containing ".ROBLOSECURITY"). If you send any of these by mistake, we delete them and ask you to change your password.

Anyone claiming to be from Tranom who asks for these is not us. Please report it to support@tranom.com.

How we use your information

  • To review your request, prepare your recovery case and support request, and help you through Roblox's process.
  • To communicate with you about your case, including replies from Roblox Support that you share with us.
  • To take payment, issue receipts and handle refunds.
  • To protect the service, for example by detecting spam, fraud and attempts to recover accounts that don't belong to the requester.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • With your consent only, to understand how visitors use the site through analytics.

We use AI tools to help organise your case and draft documents. A person reviews this work, and we don't use your case materials to train AI models.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, prevent fraud and improve how we work, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: for analytics cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records and respond to lawful requests.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work for us under contract, such as our website host, form-processing service, email provider and payment processor. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Roblox, only when you ask us to, or as part of materials you submit to Roblox yourself.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect people from harm or fraud.
  • A buyer or successor, if Tranom is sold or merged, under the same protections described here.

How long we keep it

  • Case details and materials: while your case is open, then up to 90 days after it closes in case you have follow-up questions, then deleted.
  • Payment and tax records: as long as the law requires, which is often several years.
  • Requests we don't take on: deleted within 30 days.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

Children

Tranom is for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must fill in the form with you and approve any payment. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country, without a parent's or guardian's involvement. If you believe a child has sent us information without that, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent you've given, such as for analytics;

To use any of these rights, email support@tranom.com. We'll reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first.

Security

We use encrypted connections, limit who can see case materials, and work only with providers that protect data properly. No system is perfectly secure, so please send us only what your case needs and never send passwords or codes.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. When we transfer personal information out of the EEA or UK, we use safeguards approved by law, such as standard contractual clauses.
