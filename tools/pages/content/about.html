About Tranom

Tranom is an independent, paid service that helps people get Roblox accounts back through Roblox's official support channels. We're not part of Roblox, and we have no special access.

Why Tranom exists

Getting a lost or hacked Roblox account back usually comes down to two things: sending Roblox Support the right proof of ownership, and following up until the case is done. Roblox Support is free, but many people don't know what proof counts, give up after a rejected ticket, or lose track of the follow-ups.

Tranom exists to do that work carefully and honestly. We help you collect the right proof, write a clear request, follow up every day, and secure the account once it's back. We charge a published price for that work, and we never claim access or guarantees we don't have.

How people check T1

T1 is the case agent our team built. It handles the routine part of each case: one everyday follow-up with Roblox Support per open case, each day.

Our human agents work alongside T1 on every case. They review what T1 prepares and step in whenever a reply from Roblox needs a person's judgment. T1 runs on third-party processors under contract, which may use your information only to run it.
