Questions

Is Tranom part of Roblox?

No. Tranom is an independent service and isn't affiliated with Roblox Corporation. We prepare you for Roblox's official recovery process, and Roblox Support makes the final decision.

Can you guarantee I get my account back?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Only Roblox can restore an account. We help you send the clearest request with the strongest proof you have.

Will you ask for my password?

Never. We don't need your password, 2-Step codes or browser cookies to help you, and you should never share them with anyone.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your case and email you with your next steps, including the proof of ownership worth collecting.

What counts as proof of ownership?

Receipts for Robux or Premium purchases, a gift card you redeemed, the first email used on the account, roughly when you created it, past usernames, and the devices you play on. Receipts and gift cards usually carry the most weight.

I'm under 18. Can I use Tranom?

Yes, with a parent or guardian. They need to fill in the form with you and approve any payment.
